Ribbit Rapids — Privacy Policy

Effective date: pending publication · Last updated: September 29, 2026
Draft notice: this page is a working draft prepared ahead of store submission. One item is still pending an owner decision before this policy is final: whether Ribbit Rapids is directed at children (this affects COPPA/Google Play Families and Apple Kids Category obligations). Until that's decided, this policy is written to the more conservative, general-audience standard.

Who we are

Ribbit Rapids is developed by an independent developer. If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact ryn.hermawan@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account data: none. Ribbit Rapids has no account system, no sign-in, and does not ask you for your name, email, or any personal information to play.

Game progress: stored on your device only. Your best score, unlocked hats, and settings are saved locally on your device. We do not receive or store this data ourselves, and it is not transmitted anywhere.

Analytics: none collected by us today. The current version of the app does not send gameplay analytics to us. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and any analytics added would be aggregate/behavioral (e.g. which stage a run ended on), never personal information.

Advertising

Ribbit Rapids shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, and — where you consent — to personalize them. Before any ad is requested, you're shown a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) so you can choose whether to allow personalized advertising, consistent with GDPR/CCPA-style consent requirements.

For details on what Google's advertising products collect and how they use it, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share any data with third parties ourselves, beyond what Google AdMob's SDK inherently collects as part of serving ads (see above), which is governed by Google's own privacy practices and your consent choices.

Children's privacy

[Owner decision pending — see the draft notice above.] Until Ribbit Rapids' target audience is formally declared, we do not knowingly direct the app at, or knowingly collect information from, children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the email above and we'll address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date above.
